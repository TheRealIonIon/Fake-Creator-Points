# Fake Creator Points

Ever wanted creator points, but couldn't make a rate-worthy level? Don't worry, now you can have them anyway!

This mod lets you customize your creator point count and display it on your profile and leaderboards.

And what about your friends? You can customize their creator points too! In fact, you can do it for almost any GD user.

(Mod idea by **theunrealsanii**)
